AutoCart Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

AutoCart is designed to minimize data collection. It does not require an AutoCart account, does not store retailer passwords, and does not purchase products or complete checkout for users.

Who operates AutoCart

AutoCart is maintained through the GitHub project gerbecp-cmd/Autocart. Privacy questions may be submitted through the repository's public Issues/contact mechanism while the project is in development.

Information processed locally

Built-in recipe commands can be processed directly on your device. AutoCart may store small local settings in your browser or app WebView, such as the configured AI endpoint, whether you acknowledged the first-run disclaimer, and cached application files used for offline/PWA functionality.

AI commands

If the AI service is enabled, the shopping or recipe command you submit is transmitted to the AutoCart Cloudflare Worker and may be processed by Cloudflare Workers AI to generate the requested shopping plan. AutoCart does not intentionally use those commands to build advertising profiles or sell them to advertisers.

Technical and operational data

Hosting, network, and AI providers may process standard technical information needed to deliver and secure the service, such as IP address, request timing, device/browser information, and error or security metadata. Operational logs may be retained according to the configured hosting provider settings and policies.

Retailer handoffs

When you choose to open a retailer result, AutoCart sends you to the selected retailer using a search or product URL. The retailer can then receive the search terms included in that URL along with information normally sent by your browser or retailer app. The retailer's privacy policy governs its collection and use of that information. AutoCart may prepare or organize a cart, but the consumer must personally review and complete any purchase with the retailer.

Voice input

On Android, AutoCart invokes the device's speech-recognition service when you request voice input. The speech provider may process audio or transcription according to the provider and device settings. AutoCart itself receives the resulting text command and does not intentionally record or retain the raw microphone audio.

Clipboard

AutoCart accesses the clipboard only when you explicitly use a copy function provided by the app.

Accounts, purchases, payments, advertising, and analytics

The current AutoCart build does not provide AutoCart user accounts, directly process payment-card information, purchase products, submit retailer orders, or complete checkout. The consumer alone decides whether to purchase and personally completes checkout and payment with the retailer. The current build also does not include third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDKs.

Affiliate links

If affiliate or referral links are introduced, AutoCart will disclose that relationship near the relevant link or recommendation. Affiliate tracking may involve identifiers or parameters handled by the retailer or affiliate network, and this policy will be updated before such tracking is enabled.

Retention and deletion

AutoCart does not currently maintain an account database of user shopping history. Local app/browser data can be removed by clearing AutoCart's site/app data or uninstalling the app. Data processed by third-party service providers may be retained according to their applicable settings and policies.

Security

Hosted AutoCart services are intended to use encrypted HTTPS connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid entering retailer passwords, payment-card numbers, or other unnecessary sensitive information into AutoCart commands.

Children

AutoCart is a general shopping utility and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Purchases and retailer accounts remain subject to each retailer's age and account requirements.

Changes

This policy may be updated as AutoCart adds accounts, analytics, affiliate programs, retailer integrations, or other data-processing features. The effective date will be updated when material changes are made.
