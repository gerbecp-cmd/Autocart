Before you use AutoCart

AutoCart is a shopping-assistance tool that uses automated and AI-generated recommendations. Please understand:

  • AutoCart does not purchase anything for you. It may prepare a cart or shopping list, but you must personally review the cart, decide whether to buy, and complete checkout with the retailer.
  • AutoCart is not affiliated with or endorsed by the retailers it links to unless explicitly stated.
  • AI recommendations and file extraction can be wrong. Review every item before purchase.
  • Prices, inventory, ratings, shipping, sellers, and promotions can change. The retailer checkout is final.
  • For food, always verify ingredients, allergens, labels, and dietary restrictions yourself.
  • Payment, returns, delivery, and retailer accounts remain with the retailer.
